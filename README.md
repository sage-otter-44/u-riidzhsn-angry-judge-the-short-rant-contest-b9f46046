## Entry

> My printer only ever runs out of ink at 4:59 p.m. on a Friday, right when the one document I need is due. It blinks 'paper jam' at a tray full of paper, like a toddler insisting the floor is lava. I have fed it, patted it, whispered to it, and it still prints half a page, then sighs. It saw me cry over a spreadsheet once and did nothing. I am convinced it is not broken — it is unionised.
